About SunnyDays

We are a community-centered daycare and playschool serving children ages 18 months to 6 years. Our experienced, CPR-certified educators create a safe and inclusive environment where children explore, imagine, and grow.

Our approach blends play-based learning with gentle structure. Daily routines help children feel secure, while open-ended activities spark curiosity and build confidence.

  • 🎨

    Creative Curriculum

    Art, music, and story time included every day.

  • 🌿

    Outdoor Play

    Safe, shaded play yard for daily movement and fun.

  • 🥗

    Healthy Meals

    Nutritious snacks and lunches, with allergy-friendly options.
